Methodology & transparency

Definitions and scoring rules used in the supplied analysis.

01 · Pressure definition

Late innings: ball 91 onward (overs 16–20) in either innings. Chase stress: second innings with required run rate ≥ 9.0; OR ≥ 5 wickets lost with ≤ 60 balls remaining; OR ≥ 15 runs needed with ≤ 30 balls remaining. RRR is measured before the delivery.

02 · Batter score

60% strike-rate lift, 20% boundary-frequency lift and 20% change in dismissal frequency. Lower dismissal-rate increase is favourable. Features are winsorised and standardised across eligible players.

03 · Bowler score

50% economy-rate saving, 30% pressure wicket-rate lift and 20% pressure dot-ball-rate lift. Features are winsorised and standardised across eligible players.

04 · Sample adjustment

Scores are shifted to a 50-point base and shrunk toward 50 for smaller pressure samples using √(pressure balls ÷ (pressure balls + 100)).

05 · Eligibility

Batters: at least 200 overall balls, 60 pressure balls and pressure appearances in 5 matches. Bowlers: at least 180 overall legal balls, 60 pressure legal balls and pressure appearances in 5 matches.

06 · Exclusion rule

Player IDs associated with a team named Pakistan in the competition-team-player mapping were excluded. This is a mapping-based exclusion, not independent nationality verification.

07 · Batting milestones

Batting average = runs ÷ dismissals; strike rate = batter runs ÷ balls faced × 100. 30→50 conversion = 50+ innings ÷ 30+ innings. 50→80+ conversion = 80+ innings ÷ 50+ innings. 50+ team win % = wins in matches where the batter made 50+ ÷ matches with a 50+ innings. Wide deliveries are excluded from balls faced. Milestone rates are shown with their counts in the player profile.

Limitations: All overs 16–20 are treated as pressure, including one-sided matches. Venue, opposition, pitch, match importance and role are not adjusted. T20 and T20I are combined. Auction registration, availability, reserve price, retention/RTM, squad composition and purse are not included. Rankings are a screening shortlist, not final bid recommendations.
